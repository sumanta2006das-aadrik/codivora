WHO WE ARE

About Codivora

Codivora is an independent technology and learning initiative focused on software development, mobile applications, web technologies and programming education.

Our goal is to combine practical technology development with structured computer education so that learners can understand concepts and apply them through real projects.

Codivora is designed to grow gradually—from a simple online technology initiative into a platform for software development, digital products and programming education.
