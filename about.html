WHO WE ARE

Technology meets Learning.

Codivora is an independent technology and learning initiative focused on software development, mobile applications, web technologies and programming education.

Our goal is to make technology easier to understand and more accessible for learners, students, developers and technology enthusiasts.

We believe that learning technology should not only be about reading concepts. It should also be about creating real projects, solving problems and turning ideas into useful solutions.
